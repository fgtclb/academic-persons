The contracts of one profile item. A caller that has one contract to show - the selected contracts element, EXT:academic_contacts4pages - passes it as "contract" and gets exactly that one. Otherwise the plugin settings decide which of the profile's contracts are shown: "contracts.display", "contracts.matchFilter" and "contracts.onlyValid", applied by "persons:contracts". Iterating "profile.contracts" here instead would ignore all three.
