The position line of each contract. "profile.details.position" in Settings.yaml selects the contracts - "contracts" (all|first) and "onlyValid" - and "fields" what the line shows of each of them, in order: "position", "functionType" and "organisationalUnit". Every value is an element of its own, and a contract without any of them gets no line.
